WHAT IS SNAKE?

  • Classic game archetype
  • Origins
    • Blockade (1976)
    • Theme: Walls, Worms, Snake
    • Many snake iterations over time
  • Core rules
    • Snake has a length that increases over time
    • Colliding into obstacles ends your run
    • Commonly multiplayer
  • Why snake?
    • Simple
    • Fun to play
    • Recognizable
